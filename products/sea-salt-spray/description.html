FAQs

Will it dry out my hair?

Spraymax has no drying alcohols and no sulfates, so it's gentle enough for daily use. If your hair is very dry, use a light conditioner on wash days.

Can I use it every day?

Yes. It's lightweight and washes out easily with normal shampoo.

Will it make my hair sticky or greasy?

No. It dries to a clean, touchable finish. If it ever feels heavy, you've used too much. Start with 4–6 sprays.

The spray feels blocked. What do I do?

Sea salt can dry inside the nozzle. Pull off the spray head, run it under warm water for 30 seconds, and put it back. Shake well before every use. Still having trouble? Message us and we'll help.
