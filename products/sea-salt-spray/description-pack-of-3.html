FAQs

Will it dry out my hair?

Spraymax has no drying alcohols and no sulfates, and it contains aloe vera, glycerin and rice protein. It's made to be safe for daily use. If your hair is naturally very dry, use a light conditioner on wash days.

Can I use it every day?

Yes. It's lightweight and washes out with normal shampoo.

Will it make my hair sticky or greasy?

No. It dries to a matte, touchable finish. If it ever feels heavy, you've used too much. Start with a few sprays and add more if needed.

The spray feels blocked. What do I do?

Sea salt can dry inside the nozzle. Pull off the spray head, run it under warm water for 30 seconds, and put it back. Shake well before every use. Still having trouble? Contact us and we'll help.
